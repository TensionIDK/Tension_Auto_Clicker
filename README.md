## ✨ Core Features

### 🔒 Hardware CapsLock Master Switch
- Directly mirrors your keyboard's **physical CapsLock LED**.
- If the CapsLock light is **OFF**, the clicking engine is 100% disarmed at the hardware level. No surprise clicks, no accidental desktop chaos.

### 🛡️ Screen-Edge Fail-Safe ("Safe Clicker")
- Panic stop system: Simply **slam your cursor into any corner or edge of your screen** to instantly kill all active clicks.

### 🖱️ Natural Mouse Toggle
- Full toggle support for mouse triggers: **Click once to start clicking; click again to stop.** No complex two-button disarm tricks required.

### 🎯 Target Application Whitelist Lock
- Lock the clicker to a single target game or window (e.g., Minecraft, Roblox, Discord, or FPS games).
- Clicks will **only** fire when the targeted application is active and focused.
- Built-in self-exclusion ensures the clicker **never clicks itself**.

### 🎲 Humanized Jitter Randomizer (`±` Offset)
- Avoid anticheat heuristics with symmetric fluctuation.
- Set click rate and a single **`±` Offset** to generate random click intervals within `[Speed - Offset, Speed + Offset]`.

### ⚡ Competitive Presets
- **Butterfly**: Double-click burst simulation (12 ± 2 CPS).
- **Jitter**: Rapid micro-vibration rhythm (14 ± 2 CPS).
- **Drag Click**: High-speed friction streaming (40 ± 10 CPS).
- **Normal**: Pure uniform timing.

### 📊 Draggable Telemetry HUD Overlay
- A lightweight, translucent floating HUD.
- Displays live **Auto CPS**, **User CPS**, and real-time **CPU & RAM** consumption.
- Click and drag anywhere on your monitor or snap to any screen corner.

### ⌨️ Full Mechanical Virtual Keyboard
- Visual key mapper supporting every alphanumeric key, mouse buttons (`Left`, `Right`), and modifiers (`Shift`, `Ctrl`, `Alt`, `Tab`, `Space`, `Enter`).
